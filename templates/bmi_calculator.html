Limitations of BMI

While BMI is a useful screening tool, it does not directly measure body fat and has several clinical limitations:

  • Muscle Mass: Highly muscular individuals (like athletes) may have a high BMI but low body fat percentage.
  • Age & Bone Density: Older adults tend to have more body fat than younger adults with the exact same BMI.
  • Fat Distribution: It cannot distinguish between visceral fat (dangerous belly fat) and subcutaneous fat.

Healthy Habits

Regardless of your current BMI category, focusing on sustainable, healthy habits is the best approach to clinical wellness:

Nutrition

Focus on whole foods, lean proteins, and stay hydrated.

Movement

Aim for 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity weekly.

Sleep

7-9 hours of quality sleep aids metabolic regulation.

Stress

Chronic stress elevates cortisol, leading to weight gain.
